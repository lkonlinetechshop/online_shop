Privacy Policy

Your privacy and the protection of your personal information are important to us.

Last Updated: October 7, 2026

At LankaCNC, we respect your privacy and are committed to protecting the personal information you provide when using our website and services.

This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and how we protect it when you visit or purchase products through lankacnc.lk.

01

Information We Collect

When you use our website, create an account, place an order, or contact us, we may collect information such as:

  • Full name
  • Delivery address
  • Billing information where applicable
  • Telephone/mobile number
  • WhatsApp contact details
  • Email address
  • Account login information
  • Order and purchase details
  • Information you provide when contacting our customer service

We only collect information that is reasonably necessary to provide our services and process your orders.

02

How We Use Your Information

We may use your information to:

  • Create and manage your customer account
  • Process and manage your orders
  • Arrange product delivery
  • Contact you regarding your orders
  • Respond to customer inquiries and support requests
  • Communicate with you through phone, WhatsApp, or email
  • Provide information about products or services when appropriate
  • Prevent fraudulent or unauthorized activity
  • Improve our website and customer service
03

Online Payments

LankaCNC does not currently process online card or other electronic payments directly through our website.

If payment arrangements are made separately, customers should follow the payment instructions provided by LankaCNC.

We will not request your passwords, PINs, or other confidential banking credentials.

04

Customer Accounts

If you create an account on our website, you are responsible for keeping your login credentials confidential.

Please contact us if you believe that your account has been accessed without your permission.

05

Order & Delivery Information

We use the information you provide to process and deliver your orders.

Your name, telephone number, address, and other necessary delivery information may be shared with delivery or courier service providers when required to complete your order.

We only share information that is reasonably necessary for the delivery or fulfillment of your purchase.

06

Communication

We may contact you by telephone, WhatsApp, or email regarding:

  • Order confirmations
  • Delivery information
  • Product availability
  • Customer support
  • Questions related to your order

We may also respond to messages or inquiries that you send to us through these communication channels.

07

Cookies

Our website may use essential cookies or similar technologies required for the proper operation of the website, such as maintaining login sessions or supporting shopping-cart functionality.

We do not currently use Google Analytics, Facebook Pixel, or similar third-party advertising/tracking technologies.

08

Information Security

We take reasonable measures to protect the personal information we hold from unauthorized access, misuse, alteration, disclosure, or loss.

However, no method of transmitting or storing information online can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

09

Sharing of Personal Information

We do not sell or rent your personal information to third parties.

We may disclose information when reasonably necessary to:

  • Process and deliver your orders
  • Provide services through trusted service providers
  • Comply with applicable laws or legal requirements
  • Protect the rights, property, or security of LankaCNC, our customers, or others
10

Data Retention

We retain customer and order information only for as long as reasonably necessary for business, customer-service, accounting, legal, and other legitimate purposes.

When information is no longer required, we may securely delete or dispose of it.

11

Your Privacy Rights

Depending on applicable law, you may have rights regarding your personal information, including the ability to:

  • Request information about the personal data we hold about you
  • Request correction of inaccurate information
  • Request deletion of information where appropriate
  • Ask questions about how your information is used

To make a privacy-related request, please contact us using the contact details provided on our website.

12

Children's Privacy

Our website is not intended to knowingly collect personal information from children without appropriate consent.

If you believe that a child has provided personal information to us without appropriate authorization, please contact us so that we can take appropriate action.

13

Third-Party Websites

Our website may contain links to third-party websites or services.

LankaCNC is not responsible for the privacy practices, content, or security of third-party websites. We recommend reviewing the privacy policy of any external website you visit.

14

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes to our services, website, or legal requirements.

Any updated version will be published on this page with a revised Last Updated date.

15

Contact Us

If you have any questions, concerns, or requests regarding this Privacy Policy or the way we handle your personal information, please contact us through the contact information provided on our website.
